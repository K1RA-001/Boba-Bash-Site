Essential Baking Tips

🌡️

Room Temperature

Always use room temperature butter and eggs unless specified otherwise. This ensures a smooth, even, and well-aerated dough.

⚖️

Measure Properly

Spoon and level your flour! Scooping directly from the bag packs too much flour into the cup and leads to dry, crumbly cookies.

❄️

Chill the Dough

Don't skip chilling! It prevents cookies from spreading too thin in the oven and concentrates the rich flavor beautifully.
